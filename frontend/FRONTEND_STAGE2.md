# GreenPulse Frontend — Stage 2

This stage continues the Stage 1 landing-page redesign and upgrades the logged-in workspace.

## Updated screens
- Premium Individual and Community dashboards
- Sustainability score ring
- Improved metric cards
- Dashboard hero with quick actions
- Impact context strip
- Section hierarchy for charts and actions
- Lifestyle/community pattern card
- Biggest emission opportunity card
